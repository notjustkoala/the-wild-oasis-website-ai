## Cancellation charges

A cancellation made at least 7 days before check-in receives a full refund. A cancellation made 2 to 6 days before check-in is charged the first night's accommodation. A cancellation made less than 48 hours before check-in, or a no-show, is charged the full booking amount.

## Review and processing

The hotel team reviews refunds and returns approved refunds to the original payment method. The AI concierge may explain this policy and direct the guest to the hotel team, but cannot cancel a booking, charge a fee, or issue a refund.
