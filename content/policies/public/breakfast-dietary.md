## Optional breakfast

Breakfast is optional. Its current per-guest, per-day price must be read from the hotel's live settings. The existing check-in workflow applies breakfast to every registered guest for every night of the stay.

## Dietary requests

Guests should disclose allergies, religious dietary needs, and other dietary requests as early as possible. The hotel team must confirm whether a request can be met; the AI concierge cannot guarantee it.
