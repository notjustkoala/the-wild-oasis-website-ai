## Initial review

First verify the booking, the guest request, and current cabin availability. Staff may record and follow up on routine requests with an internal note. Use the applicable public policy document ID when citing the underlying rule.

## Administrator escalation

Escalate policy waivers, fee reductions, refund exceptions, over-limit pets, and unconfirmed accessibility arrangements for administrator approval.

## High-priority cases

Mark health and safety concerns, severe allergies, and issues that may prevent check-in as high priority and handle them immediately with a person.

## AI boundary

AI may retrieve this SOP, summarize risks, and draft an internal note. A staff member must approve any write. AI cannot approve an exception, modify a booking, collect a charge, or issue a refund.
