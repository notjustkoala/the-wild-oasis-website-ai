## Eligible pets and limits

Each booking may bring at most one cat or dog weighing no more than 20 kg. The guest must declare the pet in advance and the hotel team must confirm the request.

## Fee and conduct

A USD 25 cleaning fee applies once per stay. A pet may not be left alone in a cabin and may not enter public dining areas. The AI concierge may explain or record a request but cannot confirm pet acceptance.
