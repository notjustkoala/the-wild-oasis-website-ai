## When payment is due

Submitting a booking does not automatically collect payment. Accommodation charges and confirmed extras must be paid before check-in is completed on arrival.

## Payment safety

The hotel team separately reviews any cancellation or no-show charge. The AI concierge does not collect payment information, create payment links, or confirm that a payment succeeded.
