## Requesting support

Guests should disclose mobility, hearing, visual, or other accessibility needs as early as possible, preferably at least 72 hours before check-in.

## Confirmation and service animals

The hotel team confirms arrangements against the specific cabin and available resources. Before confirmation, the AI concierge cannot claim that a cabin or facility fully meets a need. The hotel team handles service-animal requests separately and does not automatically apply the ordinary pet fee.
